Flagship Product

Wey Call

Our live AI call centre agent — replacing outdated press-1-for-Amharic systems with intelligent, natural conversation across five languages.

What it does

  • Answers customer calls instantly — no queue, no hold music, no IVR menus
  • Understands natural speech in Amharic, Afaan Oromoo, Tigrinya, Somali and English
  • Handles 70% of support queries autonomously — complaints, bookings, status, payments
  • Detects uncertainty and escalates to a human agent automatically
  • Logs every call with duration, intent, language, and resolution status
  • Provides a daily dashboard — top issues, call volume, resolution rate
  • Runs 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year
  • Deployed on local infrastructure — your data never leaves the region
