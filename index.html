FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Which languages does Wey Call speak?

Amharic, Afaan Oromoo, Tigrinya, Somali and English — understood and spoken naturally, not through a press-1 menu.

Does it replace our human agents?

No — it adds capacity. Wey Call handles repetitive queries and surge volume so your people can focus on the complex, high-value calls. When it's uncertain, it hands the call to a human.

Where is our customer data stored?

On local infrastructure, so customer data stays within your region. Every call is logged and auditable.

How does the 30-day pilot work?

Wey Call runs alongside your existing system for 30 days. Your team reviews everything, and we measure real results together before you make any commitment.

Can it match our brand?

Yes. Each brand gets its own agent personality, greeting and knowledge — one engine can serve several brands at once.

How do we get started?

Call or email us using the details below. We'll scope a pilot around your call types and languages.
